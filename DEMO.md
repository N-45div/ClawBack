Record the live app at phone size (browser devtools device mode, 390×844), with a devnet wallet already funded and the account created.

| Time | Screen | Say / show |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Home | "ClawBack turns your spare change into savings, and saving earns you plays on a claw machine whose prizes are real investments. You never bet money." |
| 0:15 | Pay | Pay $4.30 at Corner Café, round-up on, **3× boost**, then Pay. "The program sends the purchase and puts $2.10 into my savings vault: +4 plays." Click the Solana tx link. |

| 1:15 | Claw → Win | **DROP**. "VRF rolls against my aim × the prize's odds: grab, lift, chute." Show the fairness receipt (aim, grip, roll, drop tx). |
| 1:35 | Win → Portfolio | **Settle to Solana**: "the rollup commits and hands the game back." Open the holding, **Sell for $0.95**, and savings go up. |
| 1:50 | Squad | "And it's social: squad goals and cheers. Spare change in, real investments out." |
